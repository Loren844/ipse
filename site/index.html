Every conversation starts from zero.

Your AI doesn't know who Isabella is, where you were on Saturday, or that Aaron is training for a marathon. You could hand your life to an online service, or repeat it every time. Ipse is the third way.

More than your apps know

Tell your AI once. Ipse remembers.

Connectors bring in what your apps already hold. The rest comes from your conversations: your AI writes it into your memory as a proposal, and it is there for every AI you use, tomorrow and in a year.

  • Summaries of your daysA day, a week, a year, written from your agenda and your notes.

Your life, on one thread.

Ipse reads what you already have and turns it into files: people, places, days. The files you import are read, not copied, and nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Your contactsbecome people, with what you know about each..vcf file, or synced with CardDAV soon

Your rules

Each AI sees what you open to it.

Category by category, to read or to propose. Ipse checks these rights before it answers; they are not a polite request to the AI.

Health, home, location and private life stay closed until you open them, one AI at a time.

Try it: open “Location” or “Calendar” and see what Claude can answer.

Questions

Does my data go to a server?

No. Your memory stays in a folder on your computer. An online AI only receives what you opened to it, when it asks.

Which AI can I use?

Any AI app that speaks the Model Context Protocol: Claude Desktop, LM Studio and others. A model running on your own computer works too.

What does my AI write, exactly?

Proposals only: a fact, a summary, a merge. Nothing is written until you accept, every change is kept in the history, and any of them can be undone.

What if I stop using Ipse?

Your files stay where they are, readable in any text editor.

Is it free?

Yes. The code is open source, under the Apache 2.0 license.

Why does my system show a warning?

The installers are not signed yet, because certificates cost money. The code is open and every release is built in public by GitHub Actions from that code; the install guide shows how to open Ipse past the warning.

How can I give feedback or help?

Ask questions and share ideas in Discussions, report bugs in the issues. Code contributions are not open yet: connectors will open first, with a kit to write them.
