# Code of conduct

Ipse is made for people, by people who respect each other. Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in its name, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people who write in a language that is not their own;
- discuss ideas, never people; disagree with arguments, not with sarcasm or insults;
- respect privacy: never share someone else's personal data, and never ask for it, even to reproduce a bug;
- accept that the project's owner decides, and say so politely when you disagree.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or images, and publishing someone's private information are not accepted, in any form.

## If something goes wrong

Write to the project's owner privately, through the [Security tab of the repository](https://github.com/Loren844/ipse/security) if nothing else fits. Reports are read, kept confidential, and answered. The owner may edit or remove comments, and exclude people from the project, temporarily or for good.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
